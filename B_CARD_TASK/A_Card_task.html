PROFILE CARD DESIGN

ABDUL KALAM

Abdul Kalam was a great scientist and the “Missile Man of India.”He served as the 11th President of India.He inspired students with his simple life and powerful ideas.

ANNAI THERASA

Mother Teresa dedicated her life to helping the poor and sick.She founded the Missionaries of Charity in Kolkata.She is remembered for her kindness and selfless service.

JAWAHARLAL NEHRU

Jawaharlal Nehru was the first Prime Minister of India.He played a key role in India’s independence movement.He loved children and is remembered on Children’s Day.
